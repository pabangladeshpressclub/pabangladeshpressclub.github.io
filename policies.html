Privacy Policy

This website does not use accounts, advertising or tracking cookies. If you contact us or apply for membership, we use your information only to reply to you and to manage your membership. We do not sell or share your information. The public member directory lists only the name, city, organization and designation of members who agree to be listed. To have your information changed or removed, email us.

Terms of Use

Content on this website belongs to PBPC or its authors. You may share links to our pages. Please ask before reusing photos or articles. Links to other websites are provided for information; PBPC is not responsible for their content.

Membership Policy

Membership is approved by the Executive Committee under the PBPC constitution. Members agree to follow the Code of Ethics. Membership may be suspended or ended for serious breaches of the Code. Fees are set by the Committee and are not refundable unless the Committee decides otherwise.

Photo & Media Consent

Photos may be taken at PBPC events and published on this website and our social media. If you do not want a photo of you published, tell an organizer or email us and we will remove it.
